# Brag Plan: herdr-mods

## What is this app?
Mods for the Herdr terminal app. The headline one is Pipeline: queue tasks, and each gets its
own agent, branch and Git worktree, is checked, then waits for your review and merges.

## The angle
No mock-up and no demo mode: every shot is the real Herdr window on a throwaway repository
(`tiny-shop`). Pipeline gets the first ten seconds, as the headline; then each of the other mods
gets two to three seconds, one panel at a time. The brag is how much fits in one window.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Herdr, extended." then "Eleven features, one window." over the dimmed Pipeline starfield.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Pipeline: the four tasks drop into the queue, the planner orders them, three work slots fill,
  then the review column and the merges.
- Show: a screenshot arrives, the marked-up copy with a red circle and a note, then the fix.
- Git: the diff of an uncommitted change, split view.
- Files: a source file, highlighted, in the dialog.
- Since you left: an agent's handoff with a question for you.
- Watchers: the toast as the tests pass.

## Outro / punchline
The name, `herdr-mods`, "Mods for Herdr", and the features' names.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet mission control; the UI is already a starfield, so the film stays in it
- Interpretation: few words, long enough holds, slow push-ins on the real UI; no jokes, no stock SaaS claims

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 30 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #181926 (the Pipeline canvas in the recorded theme)
- Accent: #89b4fa
- Text: #cdd6f4
- Success: #a6e3a1
- Display font: system UI (SF Pro Display), semibold
- Body font: system UI
- Strongest visual element: the planner's orbit at the centre with task cards flowing left to right

## Share copy (draft)
Four tasks, three agents, one screen. Pipeline for Herdr gives every task its own agent, branch
and worktree, runs the checks, and waits for you to merge.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (ende.app, CC BY 4.0), from 0s
- Music treatment: low under the footage, fades out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read; 110 BPM; beats near scene changes at 2.46, 5.74, 7.79, 11.47, 16.93; strong cue 18.55 for the name
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the glow behind the window breathes with the music's level
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft hit when the window lands, a card sound per queued task, a bell on the final count
- Audio-coupled moments: the four queue cards; the name landing
- Restraint rule: no sound per caption, nothing over the working stretch

## Storyboard

The scenes and their seconds are in `video/composition-brief.md`. Each mod scene: a hard cut to
that panel, a short push-in on what changed, one caption that starts with the feature's name.
Restraint rule: no sound per caption; a card sound only where something arrives on screen.
